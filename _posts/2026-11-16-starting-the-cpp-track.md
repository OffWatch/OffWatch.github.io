If this is your first time here: hey! I'm OffWatch, a mariner who got into coding fairly recently.

I've used ChatGPT since the day it launched, and I switched to Claude over a year ago. I spent most of 2026 vibecoding: apps, websites, dashboards, games in Godot and Unity. With every project I thought this one would be the big one. For most of that time I was on the $200-a-month plan.

In November I took an honest look at how I spend my days. I was in the terminal with Claude Code for 10 to 12 hours a day, prompting and shipping things, and learning almost nothing. The rest of the time I was on YouTube and Reddit chasing the next AI model. A new one comes out, it's supposedly far better than the last, and if I don't switch I'm wasting my life and getting left behind. Claude, then OpenAI, then Gemini, and round again.

I've spent somewhere between $3,000 and $4,000 on AI. I've made exactly $0 from it.

So I'm changing how I do this. I'm learning C++ from scratch, and this time I write the code myself. The AI is my tutor now: it explains, gives hints, and points at my mistakes, but it doesn't write the answers. I learn by building: small projects for every chapter, plus daily drills that bring old topics back before I forget them. Once the basics are solid I'll write my own game server, and later my own small language model in C++, built rather than downloaded.

Even if none of it turns into a successful product in five or six years, I'll actually know how to code. This blog is where I'll log the progress, the bugs and what I learn.
